GRAMMAR TOPICS

Conditionals

Shart gaplar
Zero Conditional
If + Present Simple, Present Simple

Umumiy haqiqat va doimiy natija.

First Conditional
If + Present Simple, will + V1

Kelajakdagi real ehtimol.

Second Conditional
If + Past Simple, would + V1

Hozirgi/kelajakdagi noreal yoki ehtimoli past vaziyat.

Third Conditional
If + Past Perfect, would have + V3

O‘tmishda sodir bo‘lmagan vaziyat va uning taxminiy natijasi.

If you study regularly, you will improve.
Agar muntazam o‘qisang, rivojlanasan.

Wishes & Unreal Situations

Wish / if only
Present situation
wish + Past Simple

Hozirgi vaziyat boshqacha bo‘lishini xohlash.

Past regret
wish + Past Perfect

O‘tmishdagi ish uchun afsus.

Annoying situation
wish + would + V1

Boshqa odam yoki vaziyat o‘zgarishini xohlash.

I wish I knew the answer.
Qaniydi javobni bilsam.

Passive Voice

Majhul nisbat
Core structure
be + V3

Har bir zamonda be fe’li zamonga moslashadi.

Present Simple Passive
am / is / are + V3
Past Simple Passive
was / were + V3
Present Perfect Passive
has / have been + V3
The product is made in Uzbekistan.
Mahsulot O‘zbekistonda ishlab chiqariladi.

Reported Speech

O‘zlashtirma gap
Basic pattern
said (that) + clause
told + object + clause
Typical backshift
Present → Past
Past → Past Perfect
will → would
can → could
She said that she was tired.
U charchaganini aytdi.

Gerund & Infinitive

-ing va to-infinitive
Gerund
verb + -ing

Ba’zi fe’llardan keyin -ing keladi: enjoy, avoid, suggest kabi.

Infinitive
to + V1

Ba’zi fe’llardan keyin to-infinitive keladi: decide, hope, want kabi.

I enjoy reading.
Men kitob o‘qishni yoqtiraman.

Participles

Participle clauses
Present participle
V-ing

Ko‘pincha bir vaqtda sodir bo‘layotgan yoki faol ma’noli harakatni qisqartiradi.

Past participle
V3

Ko‘pincha passive yoki tugallangan holatni bildiradi.

Perfect participle
Having + V3

Birinchi ish tugagach, keyingi ish sodir bo‘lganini ko‘rsatadi.

Having finished his homework, he went out.
Uy vazifasini tugatgach, u tashqariga chiqdi.
